## What Is This

Mesh is a punk show board that lives in a single HTML file. No app store. No account. No algorithm. No Ticketmaster. Just you, your browser, and your people.

Built for DIY punk / hardcore / metal / noise scenes.

Open `diy-mesh-showboard.html` in any web browser and you're running it. Everything saves locally on your machine.

---

## 1. Getting Started

**Step 1 — Get the file**
Someone sent you `diy-mesh-showboard.html` — via email, USB, Signal, Discord, whatever. Save it somewhere on your computer you'll remember.

**Step 2 — Open it in your browser**
Double-click the file, or drag it into any web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — all work). The MESH show board loads up. No internet connection required once you have the file.

**Step 3 — That's it**
Everything saves locally in your browser. No login. No account. No one collecting your data. Your shows live on your machine.

> **Important:** Always open the same file from the same location. If you move the file, your saved shows stay in the browser tied to the original file path. To transfer shows to a new location, use the Share Code system described below.

---

## 2. Posting a Show

**Step 1 — Hit "+ Post Show" in the top right**
A form will open. Fill in what you know.

**Step 2 — Fill in the show details**

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Bands / Artists *(required)* | Separate with / — e.g. `Ceremony / Harm's Way / No Warning` |
| Date *(required)* | Pick from the date picker. Past shows won't display. |
| Time | Doors + set time — e.g. `7pm doors / 8pm` |
| Venue *(required)* | Venue name, or `House show — DM for address` |
| City *(required)* | City and state — e.g. `Cleveland, OH` |
| Cost | `$5 / Donation / Free / PWYW` |
| Genre | Punk, Hardcore, Metal, Noise, or Other Underground |
| Ages | All Ages, 18+, or 21+ |
| Notes | Contact info, BYOB, benefit info, safe space policy, etc. |

**Step 3 — Click "Post Show"**
Your show appears on the board immediately and is saved to your browser's local storage. It'll still be there when you reopen the file tomorrow, next week, etc.

> **Tip:** For house shows where you don't want the address public, put "House show — DM for address" in the Venue field and put your contact info in the Notes field.

## 3. Sharing Shows Across Cities

This is the whole point. There's no central server — shows spread the same way a zine does. You make a copy, hand it to someone, they hand it to someone else.

```
You post a show → Click "Share" → Copy the MESH:// code → Text it to your friend → They import it
```

---

### To Share a Show

**Step 1 — Click "Share" on any show card**
Either the small "Share" button on the card, or open the show detail and click "Share Code" in the bottom right.

**Step 2 — The code copies to your clipboard**
It looks like a wall of scrambled text starting with `MESH://` — that's just the show info encoded as text. No tracking, no IDs linked to you, nothing sketchy.

**Step 3 — Send it to your friend however you want**
Text message, Signal, email, Discord, paste it in a note. Any channel works — it's just text.

---

### To Import a Show (someone sent you a code)

**Step 1 — Open MESH in your browser**
Double-click `diy-mesh-showboard.html` like normal.

**Step 2 — Click "Import Code" in the top right**
A text box opens.

**Step 3 — Paste the MESH:// code and click Import**
The show appears on your board instantly. If you already have that show, it'll let you know so you don't get duplicates.

---

Import shows from your Cleveland friend, share them to your Detroit people, import their Detroit shows, share those to Pittsburgh. That's the mesh. No algorithm. Just people passing information to people.

## 4. Spreading the App to New People

If someone in your city doesn't have the file yet, they can't receive show codes. Get them set up first.

**Step 1 — Send them both files**
Email `diy-mesh-showboard.html` and `MESH-readme.md` (this file) to your friend. Any email handles these as attachments. Signal, Discord, Google Drive, and USB all work too.

**Step 2 — Tell them to save and open**
They save `diy-showboard.html` to their computer and double-click it. That's all. They're set up.

**Step 3 — Send them a show code**
Generate a Share Code for any show and send it to them right away so they have something on their board immediately. First code = first connection = the mesh grows. 

> Think of it like a zine. A zine or show flyer doesn't have a server. You print it, hand it to someone, they hand it to someone else. The file IS the publication. The codes ARE the content.

## 5. Finding Shows

- **Search bar** — Type any part of a band name, venue, city, or show notes. Results filter as you type.
- **Genre filter** — Filter to Punk, Hardcore, Metal, Noise, or Other Underground only.
- **City filter** — Automatically populates with every city that has a show on your board.
- **Past shows** — The board only shows upcoming shows. Once a date passes, the show disappears from view automatically. It keeps things clean.

## 6. Common Questions

**Does this need the internet?**
Only to load the fonts the first time you open it. After that it works fully offline. If fonts don't load, it falls back to a system font and still works fine.

**What happens if I clear my browser history / cache?**
Your shows could get wiped — browser storage clears with certain "clear browsing data" actions. To protect against this: generate a Share Code for every show you want to keep and save those codes in a text file. You can reimport them any time.

**Can I edit a show after posting it?**
Not yet — delete the old one (click the show, then "Delete" in the detail view) and repost with corrected info. Then share a fresh code to anyone who had the old one.

**Can multiple people post to the same board?**
Each person has their own board — it's local to their machine. The way you share a board is by trading codes. If you want to run a city-wide board, one person can act as the aggregator: collect codes from everyone and redistribute them to the whole scene.

**Is this private?**
Your shows only go where you send the codes. Nothing is transmitted anywhere automatically. No one can see your board unless you share a code with them.

**Can I use this on my phone?**
Yes — open it in your phone's browser. On iOS, save the HTML file to your Files app and open with Safari. On Android, save to Downloads and open with Chrome or Firefox.

**What if I lose the file or it gets corrupted?**
Get the file from whoever gave it to you, or ask anyone in your network who has it. The shows don't live in the file itself — they live in browser storage on your machine. The file is just the interface.

**Someone sent me a code but it won't import.**
Make sure you copied the entire MESH:// code — it can be long and sometimes gets cut off in text messages. Ask your friend to resend it. If it still doesn't work, have them generate a fresh code.

## Why This Exists

Ticketmaster charges service fees that cost more than the ticket. LiveNation owns the venues. Spotify pays fractions of a penny. Facebook wants your data to sell ads.

The DIY scene doesn't need any of that. It never did. Shows get put on in basements, VFW halls, record stores, and living rooms. Word spreads through flyers, zines, and people who give a damn.

MESH is just that, but digital.

**Ground rules:**

- No ads, ever
- No data collection, ever
- No industry involvement, ever
- No central server that can be shut down or sold
- No algorithm deciding what shows you see
- No account required to participate

The file is yours. Modify it. Redistribute it. Print this and tape it to a telephone pole. Do whatever you want with it. That's the point. 

**Keep it weird. Keep it local. Keep it loud.**
